## Upload. Quote. Ship.

Send Cut Ship was built by fabricators, for engineers. The founding team — Max Davis
(MAXCNC), Ben Stagl (SAIC, 20-year fabrication veteran), and Ally Reza (ChiLab Studio,
Chicago) — has collectively run hundreds of production runs across additive manufacturing,
laser cutting, and CNC machining. We built Send Cut Ship because we were tired of managing
three separate vendors for parts that needed to arrive together.

The premise is simple: upload your CAD files, get a single instant quote across all
processes, approve, and we handle the rest — production, assembly if needed, and shipment
directly to your door or your contract manufacturer.

## Why We're Different

Most online manufacturing platforms specialize in one process. Send Cut Ship handles
3D printing (FDM, SLA, SLS, DMLS), laser cutting (sheet metal, acrylic, fabric), and
CNC machining (3-axis and 5-axis) under one roof. For product teams that need printed
housings, laser-cut brackets, and machined inserts on the same timeline, we eliminate the
coordination overhead entirely.

Ben Stagl's background includes 3D printed sand molds for cast aluminum architectural
installations — including the Uber HQ feature wall — and fabrication consulting for
Gensler, Knoll, and Studio Gang. Max Davis runs MAXCNC, a digital fabrication shop
specializing in CNC manufacturing. That depth of hands-on production experience means
we understand your files, your tolerances, and your actual constraints.

## Capabilities at a Glance

- **3D Printing:** FDM, SLA, SLS, DMLS metal (Ti6Al4V, Inconel 718, 316L stainless)
- **Laser Cutting:** Steel, aluminum, acrylic, polycarbonate, wood, fabric; up to 1,500 × 3,000 mm
- **CNC Machining:** 3-axis and 5-axis; aluminum, stainless, brass, Delrin, PEEK
- **Assembly:** Multi-process sub-assemblies, hardware insertion, custom packaging
- **Tolerances:** ±0.1 mm standard across all processes; ±0.05 mm achievable on precision jobs
- **Lead times:** 24-hour production start; standard 5–7 business days; expedited available

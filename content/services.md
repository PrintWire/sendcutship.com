## 3D Printing

Send Cut Ship offers the full spectrum of additive manufacturing — from FDM functional
prototypes to DMLS titanium end-use parts. Upload your STL or STEP file and receive
an instant quote with DFM feedback. Materials range from PLA and PETG to Inconel 718
and Ti6Al4V. Build envelopes up to 800 × 400 × 500 mm. Tolerances ±0.1 mm standard,
±0.05 mm achievable on SLA/SLS.


## Laser Cutting

Our laser cutting service handles DXF and DWG uploads for immediate pricing on flat-pattern
sheet parts. Materials: mild steel, stainless 304/316, aluminum 5052/6061, acrylic,
polycarbonate, plywood, MDF, and fabric. Maximum sheet size 1,500 × 3,000 mm. Bend
allowance calculations included for formed parts. Anodizing, powder coating, and
brushed stainless finishes available.


## CNC Machining

Tight-tolerance CNC milling and turning for metal and engineering plastics. Materials:
aluminum 6061/7075, 303/316 stainless, brass C360, Delrin, UHMWPE, PEEK, and Nylon.
5-axis capability for complex undercuts and curved surfaces. Surface finishes from
Ra 3.2 µm standard to Ra 0.4 µm mirror polish. ISO 9001 quality inspection with
CMM reporting on critical dimensions.


## Multi-Process Assembly

The unique strength of Send Cut Ship is combining processes on a single PO. Need a
CNC-machined housing, laser-cut steel brackets, and 3D-printed snap-fit inserts?
One upload, one quote, one shipment. Assembly, hardware insertion, and custom packaging
available. Ideal for product development teams consolidating multiple vendor relationships
and engineers who need complete sub-assemblies, not loose parts.

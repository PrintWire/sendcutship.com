## What happens when you ask for a quote

1. You describe the part or project on the [contact page](/contact/). Material, quantity, size, tolerances and deadline all help.

2. PrintWire passes your request to specialised manufacturers.

3. PrintWire replies to you with pricing.

## What PrintWire is and is not

PrintWire does not manufacture parts itself and does not operate printers.

A quote request is not an order. Nothing is made or charged until you have agreed to it in writing.

We do not promise a response time.
